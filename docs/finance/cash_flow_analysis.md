# SalesGenie — AI-Based Cash Flow Analysis

## FAANG-Level User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

> **Module:** AI-Based Cash Flow Analysis  
> **Platform:** SalesGenie Enterprise AI Platform  
> **Execution Model:** AI-first, deterministic financial computation, human-governed decision making  
> **Architecture:** Multi-tenant, event-driven, microservices, AI-agent enabled  
> **Primary Objective:** Provide real-time, historical, predictive, explainable, and actionable cash-flow intelligence across operating, investing, and financing activities.

---

## 1. Module Overview

The AI-Based Cash Flow Analysis module shall provide SalesGenie with an intelligent financial intelligence system capable of collecting, normalizing, reconciling, analyzing, forecasting, and explaining organizational cash movements.

The module shall transform raw financial data into:

```text
RAW FINANCIAL DATA
        ↓
DATA INGESTION
        ↓
NORMALIZATION
        ↓
TRANSACTION CLASSIFICATION
        ↓
RECONCILIATION
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
LIQUIDITY ANALYSIS
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
AI INSIGHTS
        ↓

                         ↓
```

```text
                         ↓
                AI FINANCIAL AGENT

                         ↓
```
