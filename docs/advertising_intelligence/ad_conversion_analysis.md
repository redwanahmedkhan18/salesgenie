# SalesGenie — AI-Based Ad Conversion Analysis

## User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

> **Document:** `ad_conversion_analysis.md`
>
> **Platform:** SalesGenie Enterprise AI Customer Support, Sales & Marketing Platform
>
> **Capability:** AI-Powered Advertising Conversion Intelligence
>
> **Objective:** Enable SalesGenie customers to measure, understand, predict, optimize, and automate advertising conversion performance across channels, campaigns, audiences, creatives, products, geographies, customer segments, and conversion journeys.

---

## 1. Product Overview

SalesGenie's **AI Ad Conversion Analysis** module shall provide an enterprise-grade intelligence system for analyzing the complete advertising conversion funnel.

The system shall transform advertising, customer, campaign, sales, product, attribution, and revenue data into actionable intelligence covering:

- Impressions
- Reach
- Clicks
- Landing-page visits
- Engagement
- Leads
- Qualified leads
- Opportunities
- Sales
- Purchases
- Subscriptions
- Renewals
- Conversion rate
- Cost per conversion
- Cost per lead
- Cost per acquisition
- Revenue per conversion
- Conversion value
- ROAS
- Profitability
- Conversion velocity
- Funnel drop-off
- Conversion quality
- Customer lifetime value
- Incremental conversions

The platform shall support:

1. **AI-driven autonomous analysis**
2. **Human-driven analysis**
3. **AI-assisted human decision-making**
4. **Human approval workflows**
5. **Policy-controlled autonomous optimization**

## 13. Example AI Analysis

For:

> "Why did our advertising conversions decrease this week?"

The AI should generate an evidence-based analysis such as:

```text
Conversions decreased by 18% compared with the previous period.

Primary contributing factors:

1. Conversion rate declined by 12%.
2. Mobile conversion rate declined by 21%.
3. The largest prospecting audience experienced a 16% conversion decline.
4. Two high-volume creatives showed declining engagement.
5. CPA increased by 19%.
6. Landing-page conversion decreased by 9%.

Recommended actions:

1. Investigate the mobile landing-page experience.
2. Refresh the declining creatives.
3. Reduce spend on the lowest-converting audience.
4. Test alternative audience segments.
5. Monitor conversion tracking for the affected campaigns.

Confidence: High
Risk: Medium
```

## 16. Enterprise Acceptance Criteria

## AC-001

Given valid advertising and conversion events, the platform shall calculate conversion metrics according to the organization's configured metric definitions.

## AC-002

Given multiple advertising providers, the platform shall normalize conversion events into a canonical model.

## AC-003

Given a significant conversion decline, the AI shall identify relevant contributing factors when sufficient evidence exists.

## AC-004

Given insufficient data, the AI shall explicitly report uncertainty.

## AC-005

Given an optimization opportunity, the AI shall produce a recommendation containing evidence, expected impact, confidence, and risk.

## AC-006

Given human approval mode, no automated advertising change shall occur before authorized approval.

## AC-007

Given autonomous mode, every action shall pass configured policy and budget guardrails.

## AC-008

Every AI recommendation shall be auditable.

## AC-009

Every conversion calculation shall be reproducible.

## AC-010

All analytics shall respect tenant isolation and RBAC.

## AC-011

The system shall detect conversion tracking anomalies and distinguish tracking problems from genuine performance changes where sufficient evidence exists.

## AC-012

The system shall distinguish attributed conversion performance from incremental conversion performance when causal data is available.
