# SalesGenie — AI-Based TikTok Ads

## User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

**Project:** SalesGenie  
**Module:** AI-Based TikTok Advertising Platform  
**Requirement Type:** AI-first advertising intelligence, automation, optimization, and analytics  
**Primary Objective:** Enable organizations to plan, create, launch, optimize, measure, and autonomously improve TikTok advertising campaigns using AI agents while maintaining enterprise-grade governance and human oversight.

---

## 1. Module Overview

The AI-Based TikTok Ads module shall provide an enterprise-grade advertising intelligence layer for TikTok campaigns.

The module shall enable authorized users and AI agents to:

- Connect TikTok advertising accounts.
- Discover target audiences.
- Analyze customer personas and ICPs.
- Generate TikTok campaign strategies.
- Generate short-form video concepts.
- Generate scripts and hooks.
- Generate captions and CTAs.
- Recommend creative formats.
- Generate creative variations.
- Build campaign structures.
- Configure targeting.
- Configure budgets.
- Recommend bidding strategies.
- Launch campaigns.
- Monitor campaign performance.
- Detect anomalies.
- Optimize campaigns.
- Optimize budgets.
- Optimize audiences.
- Optimize creatives.
- Analyze conversion funnels.
- Attribute leads and revenue.
- Forecast campaign outcomes.
- Run experiments.
- Identify winning creatives.
- Detect creative fatigue.
- Detect audience saturation.
- Recommend campaign scaling.
- Generate executive reports.
- Coordinate TikTok campaigns with other SalesGenie marketing channels.
- Support AI-only recommendations, AI-assisted execution, and governed autonomous execution.

The system shall treat TikTok advertising as a revenue optimization problem rather than merely a campaign configuration problem.
