# SalesGenie — AI-Based Ad Spend Analytics

**Project:** SalesGenie  
**Module:** AI-Based Ad Spend Analytics  
**Architecture:** Enterprise Multi-Tenant SaaS + Multi-Agent AI + Event-Driven Architecture  
**Operating Model:** AI-First + Human Governance + Controlled Autonomous Optimization  
**Primary Objective:** Provide enterprise-grade AI-powered analysis, forecasting, optimization, anomaly detection, attribution, and decision intelligence for advertising expenditure across all connected marketing and advertising channels.

---

## 1. Module Overview

The AI-Based Ad Spend Analytics module shall provide SalesGenie with a centralized intelligence layer for understanding where advertising money is being spent, why it is being spent, what outcomes it generates, and where future advertising investment should be allocated.

The module shall analyze advertising expenditure across supported channels, campaigns, ad groups, creatives, audiences, geographic markets, customer segments, products, and business units.

The system shall move beyond basic spend reporting and provide:

- Real-time advertising spend monitoring.
- Historical spend analysis.
- Budget utilization analysis.
- Campaign cost analysis.
- Cost-per-lead analysis.
- Cost-per-qualified-lead analysis.
- Customer acquisition cost analysis.
- Cost-per-opportunity analysis.
- Cost-per-customer analysis.
- ROAS analysis.
- ROI analysis.
- Revenue attribution.
- Profitability analysis.
- Marginal return analysis.
- Budget forecasting.
- Spend forecasting.
- Overspending detection.
- Underspending detection.
- Wasted-spend detection.
- Anomaly detection.
- Budget pacing.
- Campaign efficiency analysis.
- Cross-channel spend comparison.
- Audience-level spend analysis.
- Creative-level spend analysis.
- Product-level advertising economics.
- Geographic spend analysis.
- Customer-segment spend analysis.
- AI-generated explanations.
- AI-generated optimization recommendations.
- AI-generated budget allocation recommendations.
- Scenario simulation.
- Predictive spend intelligence.
- Human approval workflows.
- Controlled autonomous optimization.
- Complete auditability.

The ultimate objective shall be to maximize **incremental revenue and profit per advertising dollar**, rather than merely minimizing advertising spend.

## 3. Core Business Principle

The module shall distinguish between:

```text
Spend
  ↓
Traffic
  ↓
Lead
  ↓
Qualified Lead
  ↓
Opportunity
  ↓
Customer
  ↓
Revenue
  ↓
Gross Profit
  ↓
Incremental Profit
```

The system shall avoid treating:

```text
High Clicks
High Impressions
High Engagement
```

as equivalent to:

```text
High Business Value
```

The AI shall prioritize downstream business outcomes whenever sufficient data is available.


---

## UR-AS-011 — Cost Per Qualified Lead

```text

```

---

## UR-AS-012 — Cost Per Opportunity

The system shall calculate:

```text
CPO = Advertising Spend / Opportunities
```

---

## UR-AS-013 — Customer Acquisition Cost

The system shall calculate:

```text
CAC = Attributable Acquisition Cost / Acquired Customers
```

The attribution model shall be configurable.

---

## UR-AS-014 — Revenue Per Advertising Dollar

The system shall calculate:

```text
Revenue Efficiency =
Attributed Revenue / Advertising Spend
```

---

## UR-AS-015 — ROAS

The system shall calculate:

```text
ROAS =
Attributed Revenue / Advertising Spend
```

---

## UR-AS-016 — Advertising ROI

Where cost data is available:

```text
ROI =
(Attributed Profit - Advertising Spend)
/
Advertising Spend
```

The system shall clearly distinguish ROAS from ROI.
