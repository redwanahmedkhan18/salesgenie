# SalesGenie — AI-Based Advertising Platform

> **Document:** `ai_based_advertising_platform.md`
> **Project:** SalesGenie Enterprise AI Platform
> **Module:** AI Advertising Platform
> **Operating Model:** AI-First + Human Governance
> **Architecture:** Enterprise Microservices + Multi-Agent AI + Event-Driven Architecture + RAG + MCP
> **Primary Objective:** Provide an AI-powered advertising intelligence, campaign creation, optimization, experimentation, attribution, budget allocation, and performance management platform capable of operating across multiple advertising channels while keeping humans in control of high-impact decisions.

---
