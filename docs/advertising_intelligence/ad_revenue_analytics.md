## 1. Module Definition

The AI-Based Ad Revenue Analytics module shall provide SalesGenie with an enterprise-grade intelligence system for measuring, attributing, forecasting, explaining, and optimizing revenue generated from advertising activities.

The system shall transform advertising data into a complete economic chain:

```text
Advertising Spend
        ↓
Impressions
        ↓
Clicks
        ↓
Visitors
        ↓
Leads
        ↓
Qualified Leads
        ↓
Opportunities
        ↓
Customers
        ↓
Orders / Subscriptions
        ↓
Revenue
        ↓
Gross Profit
        ↓
Incremental Revenue
        ↓
Incremental Profit
```

The module shall answer:

* How much revenue did advertising generate?
* Which channels generated the most revenue?
* Which campaigns generated the most revenue?
* Which advertisements generated revenue?
* Which audiences generated the highest-value customers?
* Which products generated the most advertising-attributed revenue?
* Which geographic markets generated the most revenue?
* How efficiently was advertising converted into revenue?
* Which campaigns generated profitable revenue?
* Which campaigns generated attributed revenue but poor incremental value?
* What revenue is expected next month?
* What advertising actions are most likely to increase revenue?
* How much additional revenue could be generated by increasing budget?
* Where should advertising investment be shifted?
* Which revenue changes are caused by advertising versus other business factors?

## 7. Revenue Metrics

## UR-AR-010 — Attributed Revenue

The system shall calculate advertising-attributed revenue using the configured attribution model.

```text
Attributed Revenue =
Sum of Revenue × Attribution Weight
```

---

## UR-AR-011 — Revenue Per Advertising Dollar

```text
Revenue Efficiency =
Attributed Revenue / Advertising Spend
```

---

## UR-AR-012 — ROAS

```text
ROAS =
Attributed Revenue / Advertising Spend
```

---

## UR-AR-013 — Revenue Growth

```text
Revenue Growth =
(Current Revenue - Previous Revenue)
/
Previous Revenue
```

---

## UR-AR-014 — Average Revenue Per Customer

```text
ARPC =
Attributed Revenue / Attributed Customers
```

---

## UR-AR-015 — Average Order Value

```text
AOV =
Revenue / Number of Orders
```

---

## UR-AR-016 — Customer Lifetime Value

Where sufficient historical data exists:

```text
CLV =
Expected Customer Revenue
-
Expected Customer Variable Costs
```

The model shall document assumptions.

---

## UR-AR-017 — Revenue Per Lead

```text
Revenue Per Lead =
Attributed Revenue / Attributed Leads
```

---

## UR-AR-018 — Revenue Per Qualified Lead

```text
Revenue Per Qualified Lead =
Attributed Revenue / Qualified Leads
```

---

## UR-AR-019 — Revenue Per Opportunity

```text
Revenue Per Opportunity =
Attributed Revenue / Opportunities
```
