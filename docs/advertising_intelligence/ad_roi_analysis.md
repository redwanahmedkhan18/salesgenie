## 1. Module Overview

The AI-Based Ad ROI Analysis module shall provide SalesGenie with an enterprise-grade system for measuring, explaining, predicting, and optimizing the financial return generated by advertising investments.

The module shall move beyond basic ROAS reporting and calculate the complete economic relationship between:

```text
Advertising Investment
        ↓
Advertising Exposure
        ↓
Clicks / Engagement
        ↓
Website / Landing Page Visits
        ↓
Leads
        ↓
Qualified Leads
        ↓
Opportunities
        ↓
Customers
        ↓
Orders / Subscriptions
        ↓
Revenue
        ↓
Gross Profit
        ↓
Net Contribution
        ↓
ROI
        ↓
Incremental ROI
```

The system shall answer:

* How much revenue was generated by advertising?
* How much profit was generated?
* How much did each advertising campaign return?
* Which channels have the highest ROI?
* Which campaigns have the highest ROI?
* Which advertisements have the highest ROI?
* Which audiences produce the best financial returns?
* Which products generate the best advertising returns?
* Which campaigns generate revenue but destroy profit?
* Which campaigns have positive ROAS but negative ROI?
* Which campaigns should be scaled?
* Which campaigns should be reduced?
* Which campaigns should be paused?
* How much additional ROI could be generated through budget reallocation?
* What will future advertising ROI look like?
* What is the expected ROI under different budget scenarios?
* How much of the measured return is attributed versus incremental?
* Which advertising investments create the highest long-term customer value?

## 3. ROI Definition

The system shall distinguish multiple return concepts.

## 3.1 Advertising ROI

Where appropriate:

```text
ROI =
(Net Profit Attributable to Advertising - Advertising Investment)
/
Advertising Investment
```

---

## 3.2 Revenue-Based ROI

Where profit information is unavailable:

```text
Revenue Return Ratio =
Attributed Revenue
/
Advertising Investment
```

The UI shall not label this metric as true financial ROI.

---

## 3.3 ROAS

```text
ROAS =
Attributed Revenue
/
Advertising Spend
```

ROAS and ROI shall always be presented as distinct metrics.

---

## 3.4 Incremental ROI

Where causal evidence exists:

```text
Incremental ROI =
Incremental Profit
/
Incremental Advertising Investment
```
