## FAANG-Level User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

### AI + Human + Hybrid Customer Satisfaction Management

---

## 1. Document Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

The SalesGenie Customer Satisfaction Intelligence Platform shall provide an enterprise-grade system for measuring, understanding, predicting, and improving customer satisfaction across AI-powered, human-powered, and hybrid customer-support interactions.

The platform shall transform customer feedback and support interaction data into actionable intelligence across:

- CSAT
- NPS
- CES
- Customer sentiment
- Customer effort
- Customer loyalty
- Customer complaints
- Customer experience
- AI support quality
- Human support quality
- Hybrid support quality
- Ticket resolution
- First Contact Resolution
- First Response Time
- Resolution Time
- SLA compliance
- Escalations
- Repeat contacts
- Channel experience
- Product experience
- Knowledge-base effectiveness
- Agent performance
- Customer health
- Churn risk
- Revenue risk
- Support cost
- AI effectiveness

The platform shall go beyond collecting survey responses. It shall connect explicit customer feedback with actual behavioral and operational data to determine **why customers are satisfied or dissatisfied and what SalesGenie should do next**.

---

## 2. Product Vision

SalesGenie shall answer:

- How satisfied are our customers?
- Why are customers satisfied or dissatisfied?
- Which customers are unhappy?
- Which customers are becoming dissatisfied?
- Which support interactions create satisfaction?
- Which interactions create frustration?
- Is AI support improving customer satisfaction?
- Is human support improving customer satisfaction?
- Is hybrid support better than AI-only or human-only support?
- Which agents generate the highest customer satisfaction?
- Which AI agents generate the highest customer satisfaction?
- Which channels produce the best customer experience?
- Which products generate the most complaints?
- Which issues generate the lowest CSAT?
- Which customers repeatedly contact support?
- Which customers are at risk of churn because of poor support?
- Which SLA breaches affect customer satisfaction?
- Which knowledge gaps cause dissatisfaction?
- Which AI responses cause frustration?
- What actions can improve customer satisfaction?
- Which interventions should be automated?
- Which interventions require human approval?
