# SalesGenie — Support Analytics Platform

## FAANG-Level User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

### AI + Human + Hybrid Support Analytics

---

## 1. Document Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

The SalesGenie Support Analytics Platform shall provide an enterprise-grade analytics and intelligence layer for measuring, understanding, predicting, and optimizing customer-support operations across AI agents, human agents, and hybrid AI-human workflows.

The platform shall transform raw support activity into actionable intelligence across:

- Customers
- Organizations
- Tickets
- Conversations
- AI agents
- Human agents
- Support teams
- Channels
- Queues
- Knowledge bases
- Workflows
- SLAs
- Escalations
- Customer sentiment
- Customer satisfaction
- Resolution quality
- Support costs
- Agent productivity
- AI effectiveness
- Operational capacity
- Customer experience
- Business outcomes

The system shall not rely solely on aggregated metrics. It shall provide drill-down analytics, causal analysis, anomaly detection, predictive analytics, recommendations, and human-governed automation.

---

SalesGenie Support Analytics shall answer:

- How much support volume are we receiving?

## 7.7 AHT Analytics

The platform shall calculate:

```text
AHT =
(Talk Time + Hold Time + After-Call Work) / Number of Interactions
```

AHT shall be segmented by:

* AI
* Human
* Hybrid
* Channel
* Customer
* Product
* Issue
* Agent
* Team

AHT shall never be treated as an isolated optimization target.

The system shall correlate AHT with:

* FCR
* CSAT
* Resolution quality
* Repeat contacts

to prevent agents or AI systems from being rewarded for prematurely closing difficult cases.

## 36. Core Product Principle

SalesGenie Support Analytics shall not be a dashboard that merely displays historical numbers.

It shall function as an intelligent support operations system:

```text
COLLECT
   ↓
NORMALIZE
   ↓
MEASURE
   ↓
SEGMENT
   ↓
VISUALIZE
   ↓
COMPARE
   ↓
DETECT
   ↓
EXPLAIN
   ↓
PREDICT
   ↓
RECOMMEND
   ↓
HUMAN REVIEW
   ↓
AUTOMATE
   ↓
MEASURE OUTCOME
   ↓
LEARN
   ↓
OPTIMIZE
```

The platform shall maintain separate but comparable analytics for AI, human, and hybrid support. This separation is essential because AI-handled and human-handled interactions can have materially different complexity, latency, cost, and resolution characteristics. Blended metrics alone shall not be treated as sufficient evidence of support performance.

The ultimate objective is to make SalesGenie capable of moving from:

**Support Monitoring → Support Intelligence → Predictive Support Operations → Prescriptive Support Optimization → Autonomous, Human-Governed Support Optimization.**
