# SalesGenie — AI Marketing Strategy Agent

## FAANG-Level User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

> **Scope:** AI-only Marketing Strategy Agent for SalesGenie.
>
> **Objective:** Provide an autonomous, enterprise-grade strategic intelligence layer that converts business objectives, market intelligence, customer data, competitive intelligence, historical performance, product information, and available resources into measurable marketing strategies, channel strategies, positioning, campaign strategies, content strategies, audience strategies, budget strategies, experimentation plans, and continuously optimized execution plans.
>
> **Core principle:** The agent shall not merely generate marketing plans. It shall reason from business objectives and evidence, evaluate strategic alternatives, model expected outcomes, identify risks, coordinate with specialized SalesGenie AI agents, and continuously update strategy based on observed results.

## 10. Enterprise Acceptance Criteria

## AC-001

The AI shall convert business objectives into measurable marketing objectives.

## AC-002

The system shall generate complete marketing strategies from structured or natural-language inputs.

## AC-003

The AI shall use available customer, market, competitive, product, sales, and marketing intelligence when generating strategy.

## AC-004

The system shall distinguish verified evidence from assumptions and predictions.

## AC-005

Every major strategic recommendation shall include evidence, rationale, expected impact, risk, and confidence.

## AC-006

The system shall evaluate multiple strategic alternatives rather than blindly producing a single plan.

## AC-007

The AI shall consider budget, resource, timeline, product, and market constraints.

## AC-008

The system shall connect strategic initiatives to measurable KPIs.

## AC-009

The system shall connect marketing strategy to pipeline and revenue objectives.

## AC-010

The AI shall support market, ICP, persona, positioning, channel, campaign, content, advertising, ABM, lifecycle, and budget strategy.

## AC-011

The system shall support scenario planning and strategic what-if analysis.

## AC-012

The system shall continuously monitor strategy performance.

## AC-013

The AI shall detect strategic drift when market conditions or performance diverge from assumptions.

## AC-014

The system shall recommend strategy changes based on measurable evidence.

## AC-015

The system shall maintain version history for strategic plans.

## AC-016

Authorized users shall be able to approve, reject, modify, or roll back AI-generated strategic recommendations.

## AC-017

The system shall enforce tenant isolation and role-based access control.

## AC-018

The system shall maintain complete auditability of AI-generated strategic decisions.

## AC-019

The Strategy Agent shall coordinate with SalesGenie's specialized marketing and sales AI agents.
