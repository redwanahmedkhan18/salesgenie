## 1. Document Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

The AI Digital Marketing Platform shall provide SalesGenie with an enterprise-grade, AI-native marketing operating system that enables organizations to plan, create, execute, automate, optimize, measure, and continuously improve their complete digital marketing lifecycle.

The platform shall combine human marketing expertise with AI agents, automation workflows, data intelligence, content generation, SEO, social media, email marketing, advertising, audience intelligence, analytics, attribution, experimentation, and revenue optimization.

The platform shall support:

- Human-driven marketing
- AI-assisted marketing
- Human-approved AI execution
- Policy-controlled autonomous marketing
- Fully automated marketing workflows
- Multi-channel campaign orchestration
- Continuous AI optimization
