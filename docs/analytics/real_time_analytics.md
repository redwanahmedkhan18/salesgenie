# Real-Time Analytics — AI-Based User, System, and Functional Requirements

**Project:** SalesGenie  
**Document:** `real_time_analytics.md`  
**Classification:** Enterprise / FAANG-Level  
**Scope:** AI-powered real-time analytics for sales, customer support, AI agents, human agents, customers, leads, conversations, workflows, campaigns, revenue, product usage, infrastructure, security, and business operations.

---

## 1. Purpose

The SalesGenie Real-Time Analytics Platform SHALL provide continuously updated analytical intelligence from operational events generated by humans, AI agents, automated workflows, integrations, customers, and platform services.

The platform SHALL transform streaming events into:

- Real-time KPIs
- Live dashboards
- Operational metrics
- AI-generated insights
- Anomaly detection
- Forecasts
- Alerts
- Recommendations
- AI/human performance analytics
- Customer intelligence
- Sales intelligence
- Support intelligence
- Revenue intelligence
- Platform health intelligence

The target experience SHALL allow authorized users to understand what is happening **now**, why it is happening, what is likely to happen next, and what action should be taken.

---

## 2. Primary Objectives

The system SHALL:

1. Process events continuously.
2. Provide low-latency analytics.
3. Support streaming and micro-batch processing.
4. Support millions of events.
5. Maintain strict tenant isolation.
6. Track AI and human activity independently.
7. Track AI-human collaboration.
8. Detect anomalies automatically.
9. Generate AI-powered insights.
10. Generate actionable recommendations.
11. Detect business-critical changes.
12. Provide real-time sales intelligence.
13. Provide real-time support intelligence.
14. Provide real-time AI intelligence.
15. Provide real-time revenue intelligence.
16. Provide real-time customer intelligence.
17. Provide real-time workflow intelligence.
18. Provide real-time security intelligence.
19. Provide real-time product intelligence.
20. Maintain analytical correctness and lineage.

---

## 3. Real-Time Analytics Definition

SalesGenie SHALL classify analytical data freshness as:

| Level | Target Freshness | Use Case |
|---|---:|---|
| Ultra Real-Time | < 1 second | Critical operational events |
| Real-Time | < 5 seconds | Live dashboards |
| Near Real-Time | < 30 seconds | Business analytics |
| Streaming Batch | < 5 minutes | Aggregated analytics |
| Historical | > 5 minutes | Long-term analytics |

Critical metrics SHALL have explicit freshness SLAs.
