# SalesGenie — AI-Powered Intent Detection

## FAANG-Level User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

### AI + Human-Based Intent Detection and Buying-Intent Intelligence

---

## 1. Module Overview

**Module Name:** Intent Detection

**Project:** SalesGenie

**Primary Objective:**

The Intent Detection module shall identify, classify, quantify, explain, monitor, and predict the intent of leads, prospects, buyers, contacts, accounts, and opportunities using a combination of:

- AI/ML models
- LLM reasoning
- NLP
- Behavioral analytics
- Engagement signals
- CRM data
- Account intelligence
- Buyer intelligence
- Historical interactions
- Sales activity
- Marketing activity
- Product activity
- Human sales judgments

The system shall convert raw behavioral and contextual signals into actionable intent intelligence.

The module shall answer:

```text
Who is showing intent?

What type of intent are they showing?

What evidence indicates intent?

How strong is the intent?

How recent is the intent?

What caused the intent?

Is the intent individual-level or account-level?

What buying stage does the intent indicate?

Which product/service is the intent associated with?

How confident is the AI?

What evidence supports the conclusion?

What should the sales team do next?

Should AI act automatically or request human review?
```

## 2. Core Design Principles

The Intent Detection module shall follow:

1. Evidence-first intelligence
2. Human-in-the-loop decision making
3. Explainable AI
4. Confidence-aware inference
5. Real-time signal processing
6. Multi-signal correlation
7. Temporal intelligence
8. Behavioral intelligence
9. Context-aware reasoning
10. Account-level and buyer-level intelligence
11. Continuous learning
12. Tenant isolation
13. Privacy by design
14. Least-privilege access
15. Auditability
16. Deterministic business rules where appropriate
17. Probabilistic AI where appropriate
18. No unsupported certainty
19. Graceful degradation
20. Scalable event-driven architecture
