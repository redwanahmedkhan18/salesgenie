## FAANG-Level User Requirements, System Requirements & Functional Requirements

> **Document:** `human_review_queue.md`  
> **Product:** SalesGenie — Enterprise AI Customer Support, Sales, Marketing, Lead Intelligence & Workflow Automation Platform  
> **Scope:** Human Review Queue for AI + Human Hybrid Operations  
> **Architecture:** Multi-Tenant + Microservices + Event-Driven + Multi-Agent AI + RAG + Human-in-the-Loop  
> **Requirement Level:** Enterprise / FAANG-grade  
> **Audience:** Product, Engineering, AI/ML, SRE, Security, QA, UX, Operations, Support, Sales, Compliance

---

## 1. Purpose

The Human Review Queue is the centralized operational system through which SalesGenie routes AI-generated decisions, recommendations, actions, conversations, workflow executions, lead intelligence, marketing assets, support responses, and other AI outputs to authorized human reviewers whenever human validation is required.

The system MUST support:

- Human-in-the-loop review
- Human-on-the-loop monitoring
- AI-generated review requests
- Rule-based review requests
- Confidence-based escalation
- Risk-based escalation
- Policy-based escalation
- Regulatory/compliance review
- Security review
- Sales review
- Marketing review
- Support review
- AI-agent review
- Workflow/action approval
- Content approval
- Lead qualification review
- AI decision correction
- Reviewer assignment
- Queue prioritization
- SLA management
- Multi-level approvals
- Review delegation
- Review escalation
- Review auditing
- Reviewer workload balancing
- AI-assisted review
- Review analytics
- Continuous AI improvement from reviewer feedback

---

## 2. Product Objectives

The Human Review Queue MUST:

1. Prevent high-risk autonomous AI actions from executing without required human approval.
2. Route review tasks to the correct authorized human.
3. Prioritize review tasks according to business impact, urgency, risk and SLA.
4. Provide reviewers with sufficient context to make informed decisions.
5. Preserve complete auditability of every review action.
6. Prevent unauthorized reviewers from accessing restricted tasks.
7. Prevent duplicate or conflicting reviews.
8. Support synchronous and asynchronous review.
9. Support individual and team-based queues.
10. Support organization and workplace isolation.
11. Support AI-assisted review without allowing AI to bypass mandatory human approval.
12. Learn from reviewer decisions without compromising governance.
13. Provide real-time queue state to the frontend.
14. Expose review state through secure backend APIs and events.
15. Integrate with SalesGenie's AI agents, workflows, CRM, support, marketing, RAG, integrations and notification systems.

## 129. Final Architecture Requirement

SalesGenie's Human Review Queue MUST function as a **platform-level governance and execution-control layer**, not merely as a frontend task list.

Every protected AI or human workflow MUST follow:

```text
REQUEST
   ↓
AUTHENTICATION
   ↓
AUTHORIZATION
   ↓
POLICY EVALUATION
   ↓
RISK / CONFIDENCE ASSESSMENT
   ↓
AUTONOMOUS EXECUTION
        OR
HUMAN REVIEW
   ↓
HUMAN DECISION
   ↓
BACKEND VALIDATION
   ↓
AUTHORIZED EXECUTION
   ↓
AUDIT
   ↓
OBSERVABILITY
   ↓
ANALYTICS
   ↓
CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
```

The backend MUST remain the final source of truth for:

* Review state
* Approval state
* Authorization
* Policy enforcement
* Task ownership
* Execution permission
* Auditability
* Tenant isolation
* Security
* Compliance

The frontend MUST provide the operational interface for humans, while AI agents MUST interact through governed backend APIs/events rather than directly manipulating review state.
