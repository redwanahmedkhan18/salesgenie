## 1. Document Purpose

This document specifies the functional requirements for SalesGenie's Product Launch Strategy module. The module automates go-to-market (GTM) planning, launch timeline coordination, channel enablement, and post-launch performance monitoring.

---

## 2. GTM Launch Workflow

```text
Product Feature Specification
              ↓
Target ICP & Messaging Definition
              ↓
Enablement Collateral Generation (Sales, Support, Marketing)
              ↓
Phased Rollout Schedule (Internal, Beta, GA)
              ↓
Real-Time Launch Health & Adoption Analytics
```

---

## 3. Core Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Phased GTM Milestone Planning (FR-PLS-001)

- System SHALL generate automated launch checklists spanning Alpha, Beta, VIP Preview, and General Availability stages.

### 3.2 Cross-Functional Enablement Packaging (FR-PLS-002)

- The engine SHALL auto-generate internal briefing notes for sales teams, support macros for customer success, and release notes for marketing.

### 3.3 Post-Launch Adoption Analytics (FR-PLS-003)

- Real-time tracking SHALL measure feature activation rates, customer feedback sentiment, and expansion opportunity generation post-launch.
