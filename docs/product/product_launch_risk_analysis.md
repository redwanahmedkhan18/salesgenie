## 1. Purpose

The `product_launch_strategy` module shall provide an enterprise-grade system for designing, validating, executing, monitoring, and continuously optimizing product launch strategies.

The system shall combine:

- AI-powered launch strategy generation
- Market intelligence
- Product intelligence
- Competitor intelligence
- Customer intelligence
- Pricing intelligence
- Sales intelligence
- Marketing intelligence
- SEO intelligence
- Financial intelligence
- Human expertise
- Human approval
- Real-world launch performance

The system shall transform product and market intelligence into an executable product launch strategy.

```text
Product
   ↓
Market Analysis
   ↓
Customer Analysis
   ↓
Competitor Analysis
   ↓
Market Opportunity
   ↓
Product Positioning
   ↓
Pricing Strategy
   ↓
Target Audience
   ↓
Go-To-Market Strategy
   ↓
Launch Strategy
   ↓
Marketing Campaigns
   ↓
SEO
   ↓
Sales Enablement
   ↓
Launch Execution
   ↓
Performance Monitoring
   ↓
Optimization
```

The system shall support both:

```text
AI-Generated Launch Strategy
+
Human-Created Launch Strategy
+
AI-Assisted Human Strategy
+
Human-Validated AI Strategy
```
