# SalesGenie — AI Cost Management Requirements

## 1. Document Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The **AI Cost Management** subsystem shall provide SalesGenie with an enterprise-grade financial governance, usage accounting, cost attribution, optimization, forecasting, budgeting, and control framework for all AI and human-assisted AI operations across the platform.

The subsystem shall manage and optimize costs generated by:

- AI agents
- Human agents using AI assistance
- Multi-agent workflows
- LLM inference
- Embedding generation
- RAG retrieval and reranking
- Speech-to-text
- Text-to-speech
- Voice conversations
- Image processing
- Document processing
- Tool execution
- AI evaluation
- Prompt evaluation
- Model routing
- Model fallback
- Knowledge-base processing
- AI-generated summaries
- AI-generated sales content
- AI-generated support responses
- Background AI jobs
- Scheduled AI workflows
- Customer-facing AI interactions

The system shall provide **tenant-level, organization-level, user-level, agent-level, model-level, provider-level, channel-level, workflow-level, feature-level, and request-level cost visibility**.

## 3. Cost Management Objectives

The system shall:

1. Track every billable AI operation.
2. Calculate real-time AI costs.
3. Attribute costs to the correct tenant.
4. Attribute costs to the correct user.
5. Attribute costs to the correct agent.
6. Attribute costs to the correct model.
7. Attribute costs to the correct provider.
8. Attribute costs to the correct workflow.
9. Attribute costs to the correct channel.
10. Attribute costs to the correct feature.
11. Track human-agent AI-assisted costs.
12. Track AI-only operational costs.
13. Track hybrid AI + human costs.
14. Enforce organizational budgets.
15. Enforce subscription limits.
16. Detect abnormal spending.
17. Forecast future spending.
18. Optimize model selection based on cost and quality.
19. Prevent uncontrolled AI expenditure.
20. Provide transparent cost reporting.
21. Support customer billing and internal accounting.
22. Provide cost optimization recommendations.
23. Support financial governance.
24. Maintain immutable cost records.
25. Provide complete cost auditability.
