---

## 1. Purpose

The `competitor_seo_analysis` module shall provide an AI-powered competitive SEO intelligence system that continuously analyzes competitors, identifies their SEO strategies, detects competitive advantages and weaknesses, discovers ranking opportunities, and converts competitive intelligence into actionable SEO recommendations.

The system shall analyze:

- Competitor websites
- Competitor domains
- Competitor URLs
- Organic rankings
- Keywords
- Keyword clusters
- Search intent
- SERP positions
- Content architecture
- Topic coverage
- Backlink profiles
- Referring domains
- Internal linking
- Technical SEO
- On-page SEO
- Off-page SEO
- Content quality
- Content freshness
- Search visibility
- SERP features
- Competitor traffic signals
- Competitor content gaps
- Competitor strengths and weaknesses
- Competitor SEO trends
- Competitor strategic changes

The system shall answer:

```text
Who are our strongest SEO competitors?

Which keywords do competitors rank for?

Which keywords do we not rank for?

Which competitors dominate specific topics?

Which content formats perform best?

Which competitors have stronger technical SEO?

Which competitors have stronger backlink authority?

Which competitor pages are gaining visibility?

Where are competitors weak?

Where can we outperform competitors?

Which opportunities should we pursue first?

What SEO strategy should the business adopt?
```

## 5. Out of Scope

The system shall not:

* Guarantee search rankings.
* Guarantee traffic growth.
* Fabricate competitor metrics.
* Fabricate backlink data.
* Fabricate ranking positions.

* Bypass authentication.
* Automatically copy competitor content.
* Automatically publish copied content.
* Automatically perform destructive SEO changes.
* Present estimates as verified facts.

Its primary purpose shall be to determine:

```text
WHAT COMPETITORS ARE DOING
            +
WHY THEY ARE WINNING
            +
WHERE THEY ARE WEAK
            +
WHERE THE TARGET WEBSITE IS BEHIND
            +
WHERE THE TARGET WEBSITE CAN OUTPERFORM THEM
            +
WHICH OPPORTUNITIES HAVE THE HIGHEST BUSINESS VALUE
```

The final intelligence loop shall therefore be:

```text
COMPETITOR DATA
      +

      +
CONTENT DATA
      +
TECHNICAL SEO
      +
BACKLINK DATA
      +
BUSINESS CONTEXT
      ↓
COMPETITIVE SEO INTELLIGENCE
      ↓
STRENGTH / WEAKNESS ANALYSIS
      ↓
COMPETITIVE GAP DETECTION
      ↓
OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY
      ↓
AI STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
      ↓
SEO ROADMAP
      ↓
EXECUTION
      ↓
PERFORMANCE MONITORING
      ↓
COMPETITOR CHANGE DETECTION
      ↓
CONTINUOUS STRATEGY OPTIMIZATION
```

The module shall serve as the **competitive SEO intelligence layer of SalesGenie**, integrating competitor discovery, keyword intelligence, SERP intelligence, content intelligence, technical SEO, backlink intelligence, and AI-driven strategic decision-making into a continuously operating competitive intelligence system.
