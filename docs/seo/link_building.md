---

## 1. Purpose

The `link_building` module shall provide an AI-powered link-building intelligence and campaign management system that identifies, evaluates, prioritizes, plans, and monitors legitimate backlink acquisition opportunities.

The system shall transform backlink intelligence into actionable link-building strategies.

The module shall analyze:

- Competitor backlinks
- Referring domains
- Referring pages
- Industry publications
- Resource pages
- Editorial opportunities
- Broken-link opportunities
- Unlinked brand mentions
- Digital PR opportunities
- Content-led opportunities
- Partnership opportunities
- Guest contribution opportunities
- Linkable assets
- Existing backlink gaps
- Lost backlink opportunities
- Domain relevance
- Authority signals
- Outreach feasibility
- Historical acquisition patterns
- Link-building performance

The system shall answer:

```text
Which websites should we pursue for backlinks?

Why is each website valuable?

Which competitors are receiving links from these websites?

What content or asset should be promoted?

What outreach approach is most appropriate?

Which opportunities should be prioritized?

What is the estimated acquisition difficulty?

Which opportunities have the highest expected business value?

Which campaigns are producing meaningful results?

Which link-building activities should be stopped, modified, or scaled?
```

## 5. Out of Scope

The system shall not:

* Create spam backlinks.
* Automatically participate in link schemes.
* Purchase backlinks automatically.
* Generate fake identities.
* Impersonate individuals.
* Create deceptive outreach.
* Manipulate search-engine rankings through prohibited practices.
* Mass-send unsolicited messages without appropriate authorization.
* Automatically publish unauthorized content.
* Bypass authentication.
* Scrape private systems without authorization.
* Fabricate backlink opportunities.
* Fabricate domain authority metrics.
* Guarantee ranking improvements.
* Automatically remove legitimate backlinks.
* Automatically execute high-risk SEO actions without approval.

## 46. Strategic Operating Principle

The `link_building` engine shall **not function as a simple backlink outreach tool**.

Its purpose shall be to create a closed-loop AI link-building intelligence system:

```text
BACKLINK INTELLIGENCE
        +
COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE
        +
CONTENT INTELLIGENCE
        +
BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
        +
MARKET CONTEXT
        ↓
OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY
        ↓
PROSPECT QUALIFICATION
        ↓
OPPORTUNITY SCORING
        ↓
CONTENT MATCHING
        ↓
AI STRATEGY
        ↓
CAMPAIGN GENERATION
        ↓
HUMAN REVIEW
        ↓
APPROVED EXECUTION
        ↓
LINK ACQUISITION
        ↓
LINK VERIFICATION
        ↓
CAMPAIGN ANALYTICS
        ↓
AI OPTIMIZATION
        ↓
NEW OPPORTUNITIES
        ↓
CONTINUOUS LINK-BUILDING INTELLIGENCE
```

The system shall optimize for **relevant, legitimate, high-value referring domains and sustainable business outcomes**, rather than maximizing raw backlink quantity.
